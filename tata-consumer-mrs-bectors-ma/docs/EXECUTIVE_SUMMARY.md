# Executive summary

## Hypothetical transaction

Tata Consumer Products acquires 100% of Mrs. Bectors Food Specialities at a 20% premium to the target's 27 November 2026 share price. The model assumes 50% cash and 50% new-debt funding.

## Strategic rationale

- Adds Cremica biscuits and English Oven bakery products to Tata Consumer's packaged-food portfolio.
- Creates potential distribution benefits through Tata Consumer's national retail reach.
- Increases exposure to premium bakery, quick-service restaurant and institutional channels.

## Valuation conclusion

The modeled offer price is **INR 274.51 per share**. The base DCF and median comparable-company methods produce materially lower values. The proposed premium therefore requires unusually strong recurring synergies or a more favorable funding structure.

## Financial impact

Under low, base and high synergy assumptions of 2%, 3%, and 4% of target revenue, the transaction remains EPS dilutive in the screening model. The estimated recurring pretax synergy needed to reach EPS breakeven is approximately **7.3% of FY2026 target revenue**, above the modeled range.

## Recommendation

**Do not proceed at the modeled 20% premium under the assumed 50% debt funding structure.** Reconsider only if the price is renegotiated toward the supported valuation range, the debt component is reduced, or recurring synergies are independently validated above the breakeven level.

## Principal risks

- The model uses a simplified purchase-price allocation and excludes integration costs.
- Peer multiples are illustrative and should be refreshed before use.
- Cash, debt and share-count assumptions require confirmation against audited filings.
- Strategic synergies may take longer to realize or may not recur.

This is a hypothetical educational case study, not an announced transaction, fairness opinion, or investment recommendation.
