The following are deliberately editable model assumptions, not facts:

- 20% acquisition premium.
- 50% cash and 50% debt funding.
- 8% interest rate on new debt.
- 25.2% tax rate.
- Five-year revenue growth of 12%, 11%, 10%, 9%, and 8%.
- EBITDA margin expansion from 13.0% to 14.5%.
- WACC of 9.5% and terminal growth of 5.0%.
- Synergies equal to 2%, 3%, or 4% of target revenue.
- Peer EV/EBITDA multiples are an illustrative training snapshot, not live market quotes.
